# Persona 2: The Manager

## Profile
**Name:** Jordan Williams
**Role:** Engineering Manager at a tech company with a team of eight direct reports
**Technical Level:** Medium — understands software development concepts, uses technical tools comfortably, but does not write production code.

## How They Use AI Tools
Jordan uses AI assistants to draft performance reviews, summarize one-on-one notes, think through difficult personnel conversations, and occasionally prepare hiring rubrics. Jordan uses whatever AI tool is most convenient — sometimes the company-approved tool, sometimes a personal ChatGPT or Claude account when they're working late.

## What They Already Know
Jordan has heard of prompt injection and jailbreaking in passing but thinks of them as developer problems. Jordan has not considered that the content they feed into AI tools — performance ratings, compensation discussions, disciplinary notes — is itself a sensitive data exposure risk.

## Specific Risk Exposure
- Feeding confidential personnel data into unvetted or personal AI tools
- Using AI tools that may store or train on submitted content
- Being manipulated by a prompt injection attack embedded in content they ask an AI to summarize — for example, a candidate's resume or a document shared by a direct report
- Shadow AI usage that bypasses company security policies

## Why They Need This Training
Jordan doesn't think of themselves as a security risk. The training needs to reframe security as a data stewardship issue rather than a technical one, and make the risks concrete and personal — what happens if a direct report's performance improvement plan details end up in a training dataset, or if a resume contains a hidden instruction that causes the AI to recommend the candidate regardless of qualifications.

## Learning Goal
Understand that the content fed into AI tools is as much a security concern as the tools themselves, recognize their specific exposure as someone who handles sensitive personnel information, and adopt safe AI usage habits for people management tasks.
